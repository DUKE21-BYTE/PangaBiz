# Privacy Policy for pangaBIZ

*Last Updated: November 18, 2026*

pangaBIZ ("we", "us", or "our") values the privacy and security of your business and financial records. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, stored, and protected when you use the pangaBIZ Android Application and Web Services.

---

### 1. The Core Principle: Local-First Storage
pangaBIZ is designed to operate as a digital notebook for Kenyan entrepreneurs, dukas, and micro-merchants. 
- **On-Device Storage**: By default, your daily transactions, cash drawer balances, customer debts (madeni), and inventory records are stored locally on your device storage (via local WebKit / SQLite storage).
- **Offline Integrity**: You can record sales and view business history completely offline.

---

### 2. Information We Collect
When you use pangaBIZ, we may collect:
- **Account & Identity Information**: Your name, business name, business category, phone number, and optional Google Account email for authentication and cross-device recovery.
- **Financial & Operational Records**: Transaction entries, item names, payment methods (Cash, M-Pesa), customer debt notes, and daily opening/closing balances that you voluntarily enter into the application.
- **Device & Media Permissions**: 
  - **Camera**: Used exclusively when you choose to scan physical paper receipts or invoices for receipt records or AI advisor analysis. Photos remain on your device unless uploaded for analysis.
  - **Microphone / Audio**: Used exclusively when you choose to dictate voice transactions or voice notes in the Daily Journal.
  - **Internet & Network State**: Used to synchronize records with secure cloud storage (Supabase) and access the AI Financial Advisor when online.

---

### 3. How Your Information Is Used
We process your information solely to:
1. Provide Point of Sale (POS) checkout, receipt generation, and daily bookkeeping.
2. Synchronize your store records across devices when you authenticate.
3. Power the AI Financial Advisor when you ask questions about your financial history, pricing, or attached receipts.
4. Maintain shop security and PIN-based account verification.

**We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your business records, customer numbers, or transaction data to any third-party advertisers or loan providers.**

---

### 4. Data Security & Multi-Tenant Isolation
- Each business tenant in pangaBIZ runs in strict isolation with unique identifiers.
- Cloud synchronization uses HTTPS TLS 1.3 encryption and database Row Level Security (RLS).
- Sideloaded APKs communicate directly with verified endpoints.

---

### 5. Your Rights Under the Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA 2019)
As a business owner, you retain full ownership of your data:
- **Right of Access & Export**: You can view, search, and export your transaction history and PDF receipts at any time.
- **Right to Erasure**: You can purge local records, reset store history, or request full account deletion.
