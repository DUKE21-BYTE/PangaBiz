Frequently Asked Questions

Does pangaBIZ require daily internet data bundles?

No. pangaBIZ stores all transactions, inventory, customer debts, and journal notes directly on your phone. You can record a full day of sales with airplane mode or no data bundles, and it syncs when connected.

Can I use pangaBIZ on my laptop or PC?

Yes. Simply click Sign Up / Sign In. All sales entered on your phone or PC sync to the same store account in real time.

Why does Android warn "File might be harmful" when installing?

This is a standard Android security message shown for any app downloaded directly from a website instead of Google Play. pangaBIZ contains zero tracking, zero malware, and does not require invasive permissions.

Is my shop and customer debt data private?

Yes. Fully compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA 2019). We do not monetize, sell, or inspect your customer phone numbers, sales figures, or financial entries.

Can my shop assistants use it without seeing my profit?

Yes. You can create Cashier profiles locked with a 4-digit PIN. Cashiers can ring up sales and issue receipts, but net profit margins and database clearing options are hidden from them.
